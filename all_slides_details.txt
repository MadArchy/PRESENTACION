=== SLIDE 19 ===
APPENDIX
Frequently asked questions
19
Short answers to the questions investors are most likely to ask.
Why multiple agents?
Teaching, grading, searching, and verifying have different incentives.
What if the AI provider changes?
Pedagogy is separated from the model; the adapter can be replaced.
How do we know learning happened?
Concept mastery, retention, transfer, and resolved gaps.
Is it for children?
The architecture allows it, but launch requires age, consent, and stronger safeguards.
How long does the MVP take?
It depends on domain and team; scope is already limited to the full learning loop.
What is the first market?
Still to be chosen: professional skills, coding, languages, or internal training.
