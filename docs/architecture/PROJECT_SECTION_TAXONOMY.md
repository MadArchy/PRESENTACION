## 1. The 17 Canonical Sections

| # | Section Type | Category | Business Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | `IDENTITY` | Core Identity | Official venture naming, legal structure, stage, and brand assets. |
| **02** | `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY` | Strategic Vision | High-level elevator pitch, core value proposition, and key highlights. |
| **03** | `PROBLEM` | Market Dynamics | Clear formulation of the customer pain, financial leakage, and urgency. |
| **04** | `CUSTOMER` | Market Dynamics | Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), buyer personas, and decision-maker profiles. |
| **05** | `SOLUTION` | Product & Value | Distinctive solution overview, core capabilities, and unfair advantage. |
| **06** | `WHY_NOW` | Strategic Vision | Market tailwinds, tech inflection points, and macro drivers. |
| **07** | `MARKET` | Market Dynamics | TAM, SAM, SOM, CAGR, and competitive addressable segments. |
| **08** | `PRODUCT` | Product & Value | Technical architecture, core functional modules, and integrations. |
| **09** | `BUSINESS_MODEL` | Economics | Monetization streams, pricing structures, unit economics (CAC, LTV, Margin). |
| **10** | `COMPETITION` | Market Dynamics | Competitive landscape, direct/indirect alternatives, and defensibility moat. |
| **11** | `TRACTION` | Execution | Verified milestones, active pilot customers, deployments, and KPIs. |
| **12** | `FINANCIALS` | Economics | Historical metrics, revenue projections, and cap table history. |
| **13** | `TECHNOLOGY` | Technology | Tech stack, cryptographic primitives, edge hardware specs, and patents. |
| **14** | `RISKS` | Risk & Governance | Key operational, market, and technical risks with active mitigations. |
| **15** | `ROADMAP` | Execution | Past achievements, current sprint, and future multi-year milestones. |
| **16** | `TEAM` | Governance | Founder bios, advisors, domain track records, and governance structure. |
| **17** | `ASK` | Economics | Investment round target, instrument (SAFE/Equity), runway, and use of funds. |
