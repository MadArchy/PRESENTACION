## 1. Supported Viewports & Breakpoints

- **Executive Landscape (1920×1080 & 1440×900):** Centered max-960px executive card with full typography hierarchy and spacious margins.
- **Laptop Display (1280×720 & 1024×768):** Scaled typography and compact metric grids.
- **Mobile Device (390×844):** Single-column stacked layouts, touch navigation, and wrap-around header controls.
