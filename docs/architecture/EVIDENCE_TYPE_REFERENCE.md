## 1. Supported Evidence Taxonomy

1. **`DOCUMENT`**: Textual, executive, or term sheet documents (e.g. PPTX, term sheets, master PDFs).
2. **`DATASET`**: Quantitative metrics, tabular datasets, operational logs.
3. **`CALCULATION`**: Financial models, spreadsheet calculations, pricing formulas.
4. **`OBSERVATION`**: Customer interviews, site surveys, field testing observations.
5. **`EXPERIMENT`**: Formal benchmarks, A/B test results, performance tests.
6. **`SYSTEM_RECORD`**: Structured deck declarations, repository contracts.
7. **`EXTERNAL_REFERENCE`**: Industry reports, regulatory filings, external citations.
8. **`MEDIA`**: Video demonstrations, high-resolution imagery.
9. **`OTHER`**: Miscellaneous unstructured provenance.
