**Document ID:** VHOS-ARCH-021  
**Specification:** `SPEC-004 — Executive Presentation Engine`  
**Phase:** `VHOS-PHASE-004`  

---

## 1. Token Architecture

Themes provide decoupled, tokenized design systems:
- **Color tokens:** background, surface, surfaceElevated, border, textPrimary, textSecondary, textMuted, accent, gold, and status colors.
- **Typography tokens:** display, headline, subheadline, body, caption font families and scales.
- **Spacing tokens:** scenePadding, blockGap, elementGap.
- **Radius & Elevation:** card, pill, modal elevations and shadow definitions.

---

## 2. Built-in Themes

1. **`executive-dark`**: Midnight obsidian background (`#030712`) with gold accents (`#c9a46a`) and crisp contrast for executive boardrooms and low-light pitching.
2. **`executive-light`**: High-contrast, clean alpine white (`#f8fafc`) surface for printed export or brightly lit conference rooms.
