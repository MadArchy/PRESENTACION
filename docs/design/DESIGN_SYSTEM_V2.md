# Design System V2: Premium Executive Product Experience

**Document ID:** `DESIGN_SYSTEM_V2`  
**Specification:** `SPEC-013 — Conversational Intelligence & Premium Experience`  
**Version:** `1.0.0`  

---

## 1. Principles & Aesthetic Direction

- **Clarity & Restraint**: Generous whitespace, focused information hierarchy, subtle translucent materials.
- **Dual-Theme Support**:
  - **Light Mode**: Near-white canvas (`#F8FAFC`), crisp dark typography, single controlled accent (`#2563EB`).
  - **Dark Mode**: Deep layered neutrals (`#0B0F17`, `#161F2E`, `#1E293B`), low-contrast borders, accessible contrast ratios ($\ge 4.5:1$).
- **Restrained Motion**: 150–220ms ease-out transitions for cards, modals, and Command Palette overlays. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Keyboard-First Navigation**: Global `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` Command Center for instant search, entity jumping, and Copilot prompting.

---

## 2. Redesigned Screen Catalog

1. **Login V2**: Minimal focused authentication surface.
2. **Organization Home V2**: Portfolio-level venture signal cards and AI portfolio insights.
3. **Project Overview V2**: Primary executive cockpit with KPI signal cards and explainable indicators.
4. **Project Twin V2**: 3-pane responsive layout with live section navigation and inline intelligence context.
5. **Copilot Workspace**: Dedicated full-page conversational workspace with 5 mode pills and prompt chips.
6. **Claims & Evidence V2**: Interactive visual lineage flow (`Claim -> EvidenceLink -> Evidence -> Source`).
7. **Data Room V2**: Diligence readiness bar, categorized document status cards, and gap inspector.
8. **Presentation Workspace V2**: Unified narrative configuration and live 15-slide deck compilation.
9. **Presenter Cockpit V2**: Full-screen distraction-free execution view with speaker notes and pace timer.
10. **Administration V2**: Tenant lifecycle governing (`ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `ARCHIVED`) and audit inspection.
