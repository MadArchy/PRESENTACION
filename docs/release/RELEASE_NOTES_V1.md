## 1. Executive Summary

Venture Hub OS v1.0.0 delivers the complete executive venture intelligence, governance, presentation, and secure data room operating system. Designed for high-stakes investor readiness, executive pitches, and due diligence workflows, v1.0.0 combines feature-oriented hexagonal frontend architecture with enterprise-grade cloud security, strict tenant isolation, and verifiable trust governance.

---

## 2. Core Engines & Capabilities

- **Project Twin & Canonical Truth**: Canonical venture facts modeling with draft state editing, version control, and multi-tenant scoping.
- **Claims & Evidence Governance**: Empirical grounding of all business claims with verifiable provenance links and deterministic trust evaluation.
- **Adaptive Narrative Engine**: Audience-specific pitch planning (Investor, Executive, Technical) across dynamic duration targets.
- **Executive Presentation Engine & Cockpit**: 15-slide responsive deck compilation with audience-contextual styling, speaker notes drawer, pitch timer, and overview grid.
- **AI Copilot**: Context-aware proposal generation with human-in-the-loop review and zero credential leakage.
- **Due Diligence Data Room & Secure Storage**: Private binary delivery, immutable versions, granular ShareGrants, and readiness checklists.
- **Multi-Tenant Administration**: Comprehensive organization and project lifecycle management (`DRAFT`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `ARCHIVED`) with owner protection invariants.
- **Production Hardening & Operations**: Strict CSP, Firebase App Check, structured JSON logging with correlation IDs, 24h RPO backup with verified restore, and documented rollback runbooks.
